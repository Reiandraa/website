Yolanda, our music teacher Puji Lestari, and I made Sisa Waktu Bersama for my graduation from SMA Semesta Bilingual Boarding School.

The song follows our time together, from the day we first met to saying goodbye—with a reminder to keep moving forward, even when parting feels difficult.
